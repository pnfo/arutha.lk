# SEO

## Baseline (Search Console, sc-domain:arutha.lk, 2025-07-06 – 2026-10-29, v1 site)
* 33.7k clicks, 1.01M impressions, CTR 3.3%, position 6.3; 87% of clicks from phones.
* Home page: 23.4k clicks (70%), queries "සිංහල වචනවල තේරුම්", "අරුත lk", "සිංහල ශබ්ද කෝෂය", "සිංහල සිංහල ශබ්දකෝෂය online".
* `/bookpage/*`: 11,486 pages with impressions, 10.1k clicks, **885k impressions (88%)**.
* Clicks per month fell from ~3.5k (2025-09) to ~1.1k (2026-10) while impressions grew (46k → 68–85k).
* Sitemap: `sitemap.txt` with 11,680 bookpage urls.
* pitaka.lk/dict: 5.0k clicks (4.8k on /dict/), "pali sinhala dictionary", "පාලි සිංහල ශබ්දකෝෂය", "pali to sinhala";
  `intro_sumangala.pdf` 120 clicks.
* Demand without a page: "නම් වල තේරුම් සෙවීම" / "නමේ තේරුම සෙවීම" (5k+ impressions): a names section is an M4 idea.
