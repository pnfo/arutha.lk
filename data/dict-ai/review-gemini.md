A fix corrects the spelling of the same word. Never replace a correct Sinhala word with another word or a synonym, never
change a correct word to fit a guess about the meaning (හොඳ, හොරා ලියා are correct), and when unsure leave it.
Do not change a word's form: no added or dropped endings (භ්‍රෑ, පිළී, බැමීම stay), no other derivation or compound, no
"modern" or "standard" spelling of a word that is spelt correctly. Fix only letters that are plainly mistyped.
